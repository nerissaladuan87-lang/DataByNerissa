How I Can Help

Each service is designed to make data work easy — even for non-technical clients.

Data Cleaning

Got a messy spreadsheet full of duplicates, errors, and inconsistent formatting? I'll clean it up from top to bottom so it's accurate, organized, and ready to use for analysis or reporting.

  • Remove duplicate records
  • Fix date, number, and text formatting
  • Handle missing or blank values
  • Standardize column names and categories
  • Deliver a clean, final spreadsheet
📊

Dashboard Creation

I build clean, visual dashboards in Excel or Power BI so you can see your business performance at a glance — no technical skills needed to understand them. Perfect for tracking sales, inventory, or team performance.

  • Custom dashboard design in Excel or Power BI
  • Charts: bar, line, pie, and more
  • Filters and slicers for easy exploration
  • KPI summary cards (totals, averages, growth)
  • Simple guide on how to use the dashboard
📋

Excel Reports

Need a professional report from your data? I create clear Excel reports with charts, summaries, and insights that are easy to share with your team or clients. Great for weekly, monthly, or one-time business reports.

  • Organized, formatted Excel report
  • Charts and visualizations included
  • Summary sheet with key takeaways
  • Pivot tables for data exploration
  • Print-ready layout (optional)
🔍

Basic SQL Analysis

I can query your database to pull the exact information you need — sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and more. I deliver the results in a clear, easy-to-read format with a simple explanation of what it means.

  • Custom SQL queries for your data
  • Sales and customer analysis
  • Filtering and grouping by date, category, or region
  • Results delivered in CSV or Excel format
  • Plain-English explanation of findings
