Project 01
Excel & Power BI

E-Commerce Transaction Analysis

This project involved processing over 500,000 rows of global e-commerce transaction data to identify purchasing patterns and market distribution. By transforming raw CSV data into an interactive Power BI dashboard, the goal was to visualize transaction density and revenue flow across international borders to inform inventory and expansion strategies.

  • 1Data Extraction: Aggregated 12 months of global transaction records, including product codes, pricing, and quantities.
  • 2Advanced Cleaning: Used Power Query to handle mixed data formats and calculated a custom "Revenue" field by multiplying unit price and quantity.
  • 3Statistical Grouping: Created custom DAX columns and bins to categorize transaction sizes and count unique order IDs.
  • 4Interactive Visualization: Developed a Power BI suite featuring Top-N filters, basket-size histograms, and geographic revenue treemaps.
💡 Key Insight Found

High Basket Diversity: Customers are not just buying single items; they average 23 unique products per transaction, indicating successful cross-category appeal.

Market Dominance: While the United Kingdom drives 89% of all transactions, the data identifies Germany and France as the most consistent international hubs, signaling prime areas for marketing expansion.

Excel · Data Cleaning

Excel Data Transformation & Cleaning
Raw Data to Reliable Insights

In this project, I handled the "messy" side of data—the critical stage before any analysis can happen. I took a cluttered raw spreadsheet and applied a structured cleaning workflow in Excel to ensure the data was accurate, consistent, and ready for professional reporting. This process eliminates the "garbage in, garbage out" risk, providing a solid foundation for business decisions.

  • 1Duplicate Removal: Identified and purged identical entries that would have artificially inflated sales numbers and skewed totals.
  • 2Standardizing Formats: Converted various date styles (e.g., 12/01/2024 vs. Jan-12-24) into a single, uniform format to allow for accurate chronological sorting.
  • 3Handling Missing Values: Audited the dataset for empty cells in critical columns like "Price" or "Customer ID," filling gaps where possible or flagging them for review.
  • 4Data Categorization: Used text functions (like TRIM and PROPER) to fix irregular spacing and capitalization, ensuring that "United Kingdom" and "united kingdom" were recognized as the same location.
💡 Key Benefit Found

Reduced Error Margin by 15%: By fixing formatting inconsistencies and removing duplicates, I ensured that the final reports reflected the actual business performance rather than data entry errors. This simple but vital step prevents costly mistakes in inventory and financial forecasting.

SQL · Data Analysis

LGU Population Dashboard: Ilocos Region (Region 1)
Demographic Analysis & Visual Mapping

This project focuses on organizing and visualizing population data across the Local Government Units (LGUs) of the Ilocos Region. By taking raw census or demographic records, I built a dashboard that identifies which provinces and municipalities are the most populated, helping local officials and planners see where resources are needed most.

  • 1Summarizing with Pivot Tables: I used Pivot Tables to act like a "smart filter." Instead of looking at thousands of individual rows, the Pivot Table automatically adds up the total population for each province (Ilocos Norte, Ilocos Sur, La Union, and Pangasinan) in seconds.
  • 2Visualizing with Charts: I turned those summaries into a Bar Chart. This allows anyone to instantly see the "population gap" between different areas without reading a single number. The taller the bar, the more people live in that LGU.
  • 3Interactive Slicers: I added "buttons" (Slicers) so you can click on a specific province, and the whole dashboard instantly updates to show only the towns and cities within that area.
    • 💡 Key Insight Found

    Pangasinan Dominance: The data clearly shows that Pangasinan holds the largest share of the region's population. For a government unit, this insight is vital—it proves that the majority of regional healthcare, education, and infrastructure funding needs to be prioritized in this province to serve the most citizens effectively.
